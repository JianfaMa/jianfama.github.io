## Overview

This project developed an embodied navigation and delivery system for open-world environments controlled through natural-language instructions. It received the **Excellent Creativity Award (Second Place)** in the Meituan 3rd Low-Altitude Economy Intelligent Flight Management Challenge and was recognized at IROS 2025.
